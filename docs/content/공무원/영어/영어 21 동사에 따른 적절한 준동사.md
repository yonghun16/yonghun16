
## 1. 동명사(v-ing)만 목적어로 취하는 동사
주로 과거부터 해온 일이나 현재 진행 중인 느낌, 또는 부정적인 뉘앙스를 가진 동사들이 많습니다.
 * **enjoy**: I **enjoy** coding with Next.js late at night.
 * **avoid**: You should **avoid** buying low-quality pencils for long writing sessions.
 * **mind**: Do you **mind** sharing your Python script?
 * **deny**: The developer **denied** making a mistake in the code.
 * **suggest**: I **suggest** investing a small amount in Bitcoin regularly.
 * **recommend**: Experts **recommend** using Blackwing pencils for sketching.
 * **discuss**: We **discussed** launching a new AI-based service.
 * **consider**: I am **considering** applying for a master's degree in AI.
 * **keep**: You must **keep** practicing to master TypeScript.
 * **finish**: I just **finished** writing the public service report.
 * **quit**: He **quit** using lead holders and switched to wooden pencils.
 * **stop**: Please **stop** worrying about the upcoming exam.
 * **imagine**: **Imagine** building an AI agent that handles all your emails.
 * **give up**: Never **give up** learning new technologies.
 * **postpone**: We had to **postpone** having the team dinner.
 * **put off**: Don't **put off** checking the server logs.

## 2. to 부정사(to + V)만 목적어로 취하는 동사
주로 미래에 할 일이나 희망, 계획 등을 나타내는 동사들이 이 그룹에 속합니다.
 * **want**: I **want** to buy a Caran d'Ache Swiss Wood pencil.
 * **need**: You **need** to update the dependencies in your project.
 * **wish**: I **wish** to see the results of the AI model soon.
 * **hope**: I **hope** to pass the 2026 civil service exam.
 * **expect**: We **expect** to receive the new data by tomorrow.
 * **decide**: I **decided** to use Supabase for my next project.
 * **plan**: We **plan** to visit the Inha University campus this weekend.
 * **choose**: I **chose** to study in the library today.
 * **offer**: He **offered** to help me with the server migration.
 * **ask**: She **asked** to join our AI research group.
 * **promise**: I **promise** to finish the report on time.
 * **agree**: They **agreed** to sign the software contract.
 * **refuse**: The system **refused** to accept the invalid password.
 * **fail**: I **failed** to solve the complex bug in the first try.
 * **manage**: I **managed** to set up the Neovim environment perfectly.
 * **afford**: I can't **afford** to waste any more time before the exam.
 
## 3. 목적어에 따라 의미가 달라지는 동사

동명사는 '과거에 했던 일', to 부정사는 '미래에 할 일'이라는 핵심 개념을 기억하세요!

| 동사 | 동명사 (v-ing) - **과거** | to 부정사 (to + V) - **미래** |
|---|---|---|
| **forget** | I'll never **forget meeting** you at the tech conference. | Don't **forget to bring** your pencil to the exam. |
| **remember** | I **remember seeing** that bug before. | **Remember to back up** your database every day. |
| **regret** | I **regret buying** those cheap pencils; they break easily. | I **regret to say** that the server is currently down. |
| **try** | **Try using** a different CSS library for your UI. | I will **try to finish** the master's thesis by December. |

## 4. 의미 차이 없이 둘 다 쓸 수 있는 동사
어떤 형태를 써도 문법적으로나 의미적으로 큰 차이가 없습니다.
 * **begin**: It **began raining** / It **began to rain**.
 * **start**: I **started learning** Python / I **started to learn** Python.
 * **like**: I **like using** Neovim / I **like to use** Neovim.
 * **love**: I **love writing** with luxury pencils / I **love to write** with luxury pencils.
 * **prefer**: I **prefer working** alone / I **prefer to work** alone.
 * **hate**: He **hates being** late for meetings / He **hates to be** late for meetings.
 * **continue**: The rain **continued falling** / The rain **continued to fall**.
